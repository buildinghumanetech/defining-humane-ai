# Humane AI, v1.0

*To be drafted Thursday, October 15th, 2026, in Malmö. This file is the working draft until it is tagged.*

## Definition

Humane AI is defined, designed, developed so as to strengthen human dignity across values.

## Dimensions

- **Individual**: fosters deliberate autonomy and capacity to flourish.
- **Society**: strengthens relational equality, social, and ecological resilience & cohesion. Facilitates connection between human beings.
- **Governance**: supports healthy, participatory relationships among both public & private institutions and between institutions and individuals.
- **Lifecycle**: rational and responsible use of resources, both material (environmental) and nonmaterial (intellectual, artistic, emotional).
